Resetting the Claude account early (the Claude reset button, a credit refill) no longer zeroes the keys' weekly spend, their budget rules, `@@om-usage` or the Cost Explorer since-reset range; the window is derived from the clock, not from the account's observed reset. Claude only (Codex and other providers unchanged). New feature flag `CLAUDE_KEY_QUOTA_FOLLOWS_EARLY_RESET` (env or dashboard switch, off by default) restores the old behaviour.
